One line per run: which judge cut the most candidates and why, and what the owner did. No business text and no amounts.

- 2026-11-19: first run. Round 1 passed 0 of 8; the measure judge cut the most (6), mostly for a pivot equal to today's value. After one fix round 2 of 6 passed; a second batch filled the list.
- 2026-11-19: the second batch passed 0 of 5; 2 of 13 candidates passed all five judges. Lesson: for a tool with no audience, the judges pass only bets that build or use an audience first; direct sales tests read zero with or without demand.
- 2026-11-19: third run, another project. 1 of 14 candidates passed all five judges over two batches. The income and solo judges cut the most: one-time low prices cannot reach a full-time income, and custom work or calls do not fit a solo owner. Paid-order metrics failed the measure judge with no checkout to read. The owner told the agent to continue with the one passing option. Lesson: draft yearly, self-serve offers with a priced waitlist first, and record page visitors beside the metric.
